INTERPRETATION / SCIENTIFIC LIMITS:
Station-ID sites: geometry uses source station ID and currently published
station coordinates. That does not independently verify the 2022 antenna
configuration. SiDS sites: ground-station center estimated from ORIGINAL
2022 six-character Maidenhead grids; altitude 0 and mask 10 degrees are
explicit HYPOTHETICAL display values (not a documented historic receiver).
The prior 90/90 (0-degree) and 74/90 (10-degree) are per-ACE-row geometry
checks, NOT independent RF events. 100 ACE rows map to 50 distinct original
received radio frames in the archived SatNOGS extract (45 SiDS, 5 Network).
The charts compare geometry, NOT measured link throughput, packet loss,
solar-storm impairment, measured end-to-end latency, or V&V of H1.
The historical TLE is an orbital model, not a direct range measurement.
NASA OMNI environmental context remains a separate time-aligned E stream.
