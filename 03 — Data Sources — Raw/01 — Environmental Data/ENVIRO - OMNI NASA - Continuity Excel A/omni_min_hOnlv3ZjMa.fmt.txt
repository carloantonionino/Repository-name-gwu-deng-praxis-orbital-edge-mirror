  FORMAT OF THE SUBSETTED FILE
    
    ITEMS                      FORMAT   
     
 1 Year                          I4        
 2 Day                           I4        
 3 Hour                          I3        
 4 Minute                        I3        
 5 ID for IMF spacecraft         I3        
 6 ID for SW Plasma spacecraft   I3        
 7 Percent of Interpolation      I4        
 8 Time btwn observations,sec    I7        
 9 Field magnitude average, nT   F8.2      
10 BZ, nT (GSM)                  F8.2      
11 Speed, km/s                   F8.1      
12 Proton Density, n/cc          F7.2      
13 Proton Temperature, K         F9.0      
14 Flow pressure, nPa            F6.2      
15 AE-index, nT                  I6        
16 SYM/H, nT                     I6
